# Binders 0.8.0

All-day capture: Binders can listen through the day, on your iPhone or your Mac, and turns the conversations it hears into notes and to-dos. Links reach headings and files and follow a renamed note, and the iPhone keyboard corrects as you type.

- All-day capture, in Binders for iPhone. It listens with the screen locked, mixed with your music, and cuts the day into conversations: one opens with the first speech after a quiet spell and closes after three minutes of quiet. Each is written down on the phone with Parakeet, the same model the Mac uses for meetings, and its voices are told apart. Teach it your voice and your lines are marked as you. Only the text comes to the Mac; the audio stays on the phone and is deleted once it's written down. Mute turns the microphone off completely. The first time you turn it on, Binders reminds you to let the people around you know.
- All-day capture on the Mac too, in Settings → All-day capture and the menu bar menu: the same cut into conversations, written down on the Mac. It lets go while a meeting is recorded, and what you dictate isn't part of a conversation.
- Without the network: share captures from the iPhone by AirDrop, or save them to Files, as a Binders file, and the Mac keeps them when it opens it. One that also arrives over the network is kept once.
- Read them on the iPhone: tap a capture to see who said what and when, and the Mac's notes once they're written. The transcript stays on the phone for 30 days after it's sent; the audio is deleted as soon as it's written down.
- Captures on the Mac. Each conversation Binders keeps is a capture, and gets a short summary, its decisions, and the promises made by you and to you, which become to-dos. The language model files it in the binder it belongs to. They show under Today → Captures and in a Captures tab in the binder, and search, ⌘O and Ask find them. They're kept out of the Meetings lists.
- After a conversation, Binders offers to put what you said you'd do on the binder's board, and answers a question you asked if your notes, meetings or earlier conversations hold the answer. They arrive as notifications on the device that heard it (Add to Board, Not Now) and wait on the capture. Settings → All-day capture turns each off.
- Small talk is left out. A conversation with nothing worth keeping is hidden and deleted after a week, in case it was worth keeping after all (Show small talk, then Keep It).
- Captures are never shared with your team, even in a binder you share.
- Over MCP, `list_meetings` takes `kind` (meeting, capture or all), and `search_knowledge` takes `kind: capture`.
- Fixed on the iPhone: closing the Dictate sheet by swiping it down left the microphone on.
- A to-do owned by someone the notes couldn't name yet ("Speaker 2 — …") keeps them as its owner.
- Links to a heading or a block: `[[Note#Heading]]` opens the note at that heading, `[[#Heading]]` jumps within the note, and `[[Note#^id]]` goes to the line ending in `^id`. Headings match even when Obsidian left out their punctuation.
- Links to files: `[[report.pdf]]` and `![[report.pdf]]` open the file from the note's attachments. A link to a file that isn't there is faded, and clicking it never makes a note.
- Links follow a rename. Change a note's title and every `[[link]]` to it, with its shown text and heading, points at the new title once you leave the first line. One ⌘Z puts them back, and the note's footer says how many changed. Renaming a binder or a meeting does the same, and so does an AI tool changing a title over MCP.
- Fixed: one new dictionary word could turn ordinary words into it ("Whenever I add" came out as "Qwen I add"). Recognition boosting no longer swaps words by sound alone, a word has to be spelled closer to a dictionary word before it's replaced, and plurals stay plural.
- The Binders keyboard on the iPhone has a suggestion bar and auto-correct: a mistake is corrected at the space, and one delete straight after puts back what you typed. Your text replacements expand, and names from your contacts are left alone. Holding delete speeds up, then deletes whole words.
- What you dictate on the iPhone is kept on the Mac like the Mac's own dictations, in History, `recent_dictations` and search. When the Mac is away, dictations and to-dos added on the iPhone wait and go when it's back.
- Over MCP, `list_notes` and `list_meetings` with a binder list everything in it, however old, and a binder name that doesn't exist is an error instead of every binder's items.
- Fixed: in a narrow window the Meetings page cut off its edges. Thanks to @kuelin.
