# Binders 0.10.0

Folders and Distill: a binder can now hold folders, and AI tools you connect can sort your notes into them for you. A note that asks for work, such as a spec, can become cards on its binder's board, for you and your agents, with how they're going in one place. Binders for iPhone 2.2 ships with it and shows both. Typing is no longer lost when a note changes elsewhere, and the window keeps its size when a page needs more room.

## Folders

- A binder can hold folders, and folders can hold folders, as deep as you like. Right-click a binder in the sidebar, or use its … menu, and choose New Folder…. The sidebar shows a binder's folders under it once you open it with the small arrow on its row.
- Inside a folder, the binder's Notes, Meetings and Captures show what's in it, with where you are above them: click a part of that path to go back up. A new note or folder, and a meeting you record or import, go in the folder you're in, however you start it: ⌘N, ⌘P, ⌥⌘N, the meeting shortcut, the menu bar or a `binders://meeting` link. So does a capture you open with Open a Binders File… on its Captures page; what All-day capture hears arrives where it always has, until it's read. ⌘O shows which folder a note or meeting is in. The Overview and the board stay the binder's.
- Drag a note, meeting, capture or folder onto a folder, in the sidebar or in a list, or onto a binder for its top, or choose Move to in its menu. The binder menu at the bottom of a note moves it between folders too, and shows where it is, such as "Work › DevOps".
- Several notes, meetings or captures at once: ⌘-click or ⇧-click them in their list, or press ⌘A, then choose Move to Folder (with New Folder…), Move to Binder or Delete, on the page or in the right-click menu, or drag them onto a folder. One Undo puts the move back. Each keeps its own rules: a teammate's meeting stays where it is, and one that's still recording stays in its binder, and the page says which stayed and why.
- Deleting a meeting or capture can be undone now, as a note's can, unless it's shared with your team.
- Deleting a folder asks first when something is in it, and what it held moves up to where the folder was: nothing goes to the trash because its folder went. Undo puts the folder back.
- Tidy into Folders…, in a binder's … menu, finds titles at the top of the binder that start with the same words before |, · or - ("DevOps | Pipeline cleanup", "Social post · Launch week"), in two notes or more, and offers a folder for each, with those words taken off the titles. Dates, numbers and anything longer than three words are left alone. Untick what should stay as it is; nothing changes until you choose Apply. Links to those notes follow their new titles, and one Undo puts it all back.
- A template can name a folder, and a note made from it is filed there. On a note in a folder, Save as a Template for that folder does it for you.
- Search results and Ask's sources say which folder each one is in. On a binder's Knowledge tab inside a folder, Only in keeps search and Ask to that folder and the folders inside it.
- In a shared binder, everyone sees the same folders, and anyone in it can make, rename, move or delete them. In the team folder, a note in a folder is kept in the matching subfolder of Notes, so it reads the same in Obsidian. While a teammate still has Binders 0.9.0 or earlier, notes stay where they are and their folder is kept in each note's file: Settings → Team → Folders says who it waits for.

## Distill: from a note to work on the board

- A note that asks for work, such as a spec your team wrote together, can become cards on its binder's board, each meant for you or one of your agents. Choose Distill This Note in its … menu, or when you right-click it in the notes list. A note from the new Spec template has it on already.
- Distill, on the note's Work bar, has your language model (Settings → AI) read the note and propose work items: what to do, a short brief, what done looks like, the section it came from, what it waits for, a rough size and who it's meant for: one of your agents, a teammate it names, or you when your decision model is sure no agent fits. Or choose one of your agents under Distill: it gets a card asking it to read the note and send its items back.
- You review them first. Change anything; merge, split, drop or reorder items; say what each waits for and who it's for, with a link to each item's section. Place on Board puts the ticked ones in Ready, or Backlog. Nothing reaches the board before that, and the rest wait for later.
- Cards can wait for each other, and the board shows Waits for on a card that does. An agent can't take a card until every card it waits for is done (you still can), and when the last one is done, Binders hands the card to its agent.
- See how it's going. The Work bar says "7 of 12 done · 3 in progress · 1 waits on you · 1 not started", and the Work panel beside the note shows every card, its column, who has it, what its agent says it's doing and its last update. Both are in a note's own window too. Report tells you what finished since you last looked, what's in progress, what waits on whom, what's held up and what's not started. Copy as Markdown pastes it anywhere, and Write It Up has your model write it as a paragraph, kept only when its numbers match the report's. Ask "how's the checkout spec going?" in Command Mode or Ask. Each binder's Overview lists its specs with their progress, and a notification says when it's your turn in one, when one of its cards is blocked on you, and when one is done.
- When a section changes after its cards were made, each card from it says who changed it, when and how much, with the lines that changed, and the agent working on it is told which section changed and where to read it. It gets the changed lines only when you made the change, or a teammate did and Settings → MCP lets that agent read teammates' items. A change another agent made reaches it only after you look, with Looked at It on the card or Make It Mine on the note, and even then it's told to read the section, never given the other agent's words. Hover a heading to see who last changed it, when, and its cards.
- History keeps every version of a note with Distill on, with who made it: you, a teammate, or an agent and whose agent it is. For every note, History now keeps a version for each time you edit it, until you leave the note or after 10 minutes without a change, and each version says the sections it touched and how many lines it added and removed. Compare any two, name one ("Approved"), or put one back, which adds a version rather than taking any away.
- A long note is read a few sections at a time, so the end of a long spec or status note is read too, and the Work bar says which part it's on. When the model's reply has no list of work items, Binders asks once more, then says what happened, shows how the reply began, and offers to Distill with one of your agents. Models that think before they answer no longer lose their list to their thinking, and with Ollama or llama.cpp the parts are sized to what the server reads.
- Distill holds the model's reply to a list of work items, with MLX, Ollama and servers such as llama.cpp, LM Studio and vLLM, so a reply can no longer come back as prose or cut off. Settings → AI has a Model for Distill, with Qwen 3.8 27B suggested: a larger model finds more of the work and says better who each item is for, while dictation keeps the quicker one. Binders keeps both in memory when your Mac has room.
- Distill Again proposes only what's new or changed since last time, offers to update the briefs of cards whose section changed and to close cards whose section is gone, and never makes the same card twice.
- Agents may place work items, on a binder's board and off at first, lets an agent put its own items on the board without your review: in Ready, meant for the agents it picked and waiting for each other as it proposed, so agents take them in order, each as its own start switch allows.

## Binders for iPhone 2.2

- Settings → iPhone shows on every Mac now. Pair your iPhone there, at home or away through your VPN.
- Folders. A binder's Notes show its folders first: tap one to go in, and a note you make there, with the new-note button or by dictating, goes in it. With Binders 0.9.0 or earlier on your Mac, a binder's notes show as before.
- Distill. A note with Distill on shows its Work section: the bar, each card with its column, who has it and its last update, and Report, with Copy as Markdown. A binder's Work list, at the top of its notes, shows its specs and how far along each is. These need Binders 0.10.0 on your Mac.

## For developers: MCP and automations

- Folders over MCP: `list_folders`, `create_folder` (a path such as `Ops/DevOps` makes each folder in it that's missing), `rename_folder`, and `move_item`, which moves a note, meeting, capture or folder, or several at once, into a folder or to the top of its binder. No tool deletes a folder. So you can ask Claude Code, or another AI tool you connect, to sort a binder's notes into folders.
- `list_notes` takes `folder`, `include_subfolders` and `offset` (to page through every note), `list_meetings` takes `folder` and `include_subfolders`, `add_note` takes `folder`, and `search_knowledge` and `ask_knowledge` take `folder` and `binder`. Notes, meetings, search results and Ask's sources carry `folder` (its path) and `folder_id`.
- Distill over MCP: `distill_note` has Binders' model propose work items for a note, `propose_work_items` sends an agent's own, `note_progress` says how a note's work is going, and `get_note_history` and `get_note_version` give a note's versions, who made each and what changed. `note_progress` gives each card's `last_update_at` and `waits_on`. A run Binders starts for a card also gets `propose_work_items`, `get_note_history` and `get_note_version`, for its own card's note only.
- `list_tasks` and `get_task` give `waits_for` and `distilled_from`, and `get_note` and `list_notes` say `distill: true`. An agent can't claim, move or finish a card that still waits for another: "This card waits for “…”, which isn't done yet". After you update Binders, restart your AI tool or reconnect its Binders server.

## Fixes

- Fixed: typing in a note could be lost when the note changed elsewhere, such as an agent's or a teammate's change, in the moment before the editor saved it. Both are kept now, and your cursor stays with the words around it; where you both changed the same words, theirs stay and yours follow them.
- Fixed: the window grew past the edge of the screen when a page needed more room than it had, and the buttons at the bottom of a note went out of reach. The window keeps its size now, and pages fit themselves to it: where room runs short, buttons show as icons, the editor's less-used buttons move into a menu, and the column beside a note narrows, then hides until there's room.
- Fixed: undoing a rename's changes to links in other notes now shows in those notes' History.
- Fixed: Claude Code runs Binders starts for a card now work on a Mac whose organization manages Claude Code's MCP settings, with as much of Binders' setup as those settings allow. When they keep Binders' tools out of a run, the card says so, and what you can do instead.
