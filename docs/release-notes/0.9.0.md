# Binders 0.9.0

Agents: Binders can now start Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or GitHub Copilot CLI for a card on your boards, and you talk with the agent on the card. Binders for iPhone 2.1 ships with it. It listens for things you say to yourself and can hand them straight to an agent in Auto mode, reaches your Mac over any VPN, and adds Siri, widgets and a Live Activity. Settings fit on six pages, and both apps have a new look.

## Agents and the board

- Agents Binders starts for you. Settings → Agents → Add Agent sets up Claude Code, or Codex, OpenCode or GitHub Copilot CLI when they're on your Mac, each in a repository you choose. Each run works in a git worktree Binders makes for the card, with the tool's own checks on and Binders' tools for that card only. Codex runs in its own sandbox with no network, and OpenCode and Copilot CLI only read and edit files. None of them can read your SSH and GPG keys or your gh, npm, cloud and Docker sign-ins. When a run stops, Binders commits what it changed on the card's branch and puts what it said last on the card. A run that fails leaves the card in Blocked with why. An agent can also be a command, a web hook or a Shortcut.
- For a Claude Code agent, Permissions say what a run may do with nobody there to answer its questions. Post to the Claude Code session open in this repository, off until you turn it on, hands the card to a session you have open instead of starting a run. Binders starts Codex, OpenCode and Copilot CLI only at versions it has checked: after one updates, choose Check in its agent's settings once. These three send the card to whoever runs their model, and the privacy notes say who that is.
- A card opens in its own window: the conversation with whoever works on it, with its description and links beside it. What it needs from you waits above the box you type in: an agent's question, a request for your approval, or finished work with Accept and Send Back. ⌘Return sends, and Return starts a new line.
- Your words reach the agent. A comment on a card an agent is working on wakes it, as your answer does, and Send Back sends your words with the card. An agent's question also shows as a notification on your Mac, and on your iPhone while Binders is open on it, with a box to answer it. A card waiting on your answer or your review stays with its agent, and on the Mac, Take Over gives you any card that isn't done.
- Agents draft first. Unless you turn on an agent's Can send, post or buy for you, it's told to ask your approval before it sends, posts or buys anything. Its request waits on the card with exactly who it goes to and what it says, above Approve and Decline, and each decision leaves a receipt on the card. Your iPhone's Lock Screen shows only who asks and what.
- Each agent's settings say what it takes on and where its cards go: What it's for, Sends cards to, and Kinds of work. The first time a card goes to an agent, Binders adds From your binders: up to three short passages from that binder's notes and meetings that match it, never a transcript. Each agent has a switch for it.
- Decision model, in Settings → AI under Language model. For a card or a to-do that doesn't say where it goes, it picks the binder and the agent whose work it is, and Give to names that agent. Binders suggests Tev1 0.8B, but can't download it until its makers publish the license for its weights. You can download another MLX model from Hugging Face, use a model folder you have, or use a decision server running on this Mac. Measure scores a model on made-up cards that come with Binders, never on yours. Off sorts without one.
- Start on cards Binders sends it. Turn it on for an agent, and a card you type into Ready, a to-do you add in Command Mode, or a "Binders, …" your iPhone hears you say on your own goes to that agent at once when it's clearly its work. The card says so, with Stop. It needs a decision model that can start agents, and Settings → AI says whether yours can.
- Command Mode puts a to-do in the binder you name, or, with a decision model, in the binder it fits, and offers Give to when there's an agent whose work it is. Ask it "What's happening with my work streams?" and it answers from your boards, aloud. Settings → AI → Read answers aloud turns the voice off.
- Work and Agents, new in the sidebar. Work shows every board at once: what waits on you, who is working on what, and what's next. Agents shows each agent with what it holds and how its last wake went, and Pause All Agents. Needs you, at the top of Home and Today, gathers agents' questions and requests, work to check and captures to look at. While a run Binders started works on a card, a line under its title says what it's on, such as "Claude Code: Running the tests", and What's happening shows it on the Mac and the iPhone.
- What agents write is marked as theirs. A note an AI tool wrote or changed says so above it ("Claude Code wrote this note."), in the notes list and in search, and to-dos say who added them ("Added by Claude Code"). Ask, Command Mode and answers to what you say near your iPhone leave those notes out, so an agent's words never steer an answer as if they were yours. See What Changed shows what it changed, and Make It Mine clears the mark. Show Changes Since, in a note's history, marks what changed since any version.
- Put on Board, at the bottom of a note or when you right-click it in the notes list, makes a card in Backlog with the note's title, its first few lines and a link back. On the iPhone it's under … on a note.
- The board fits the window: when space is short, Backlog and Done fold into narrow strips with their counts. Cards and to-dos have Move to, and each binder has What goes here, a few words Binders goes by when it sorts new cards.

## Binders for iPhone 2.1

- Things you say to yourself. Turn on Listen for things you say to yourself, under Said to yourself on the capture screen. While All-day capture listens, Binders then keeps short things you say on your own: anything that starts with "Binders", and, once it knows your voice, things that sound like something to do or to look up, such as "I need to book the dentist". Your Mac gets the words, never audio, and adds it, offers it to you, or answers it from your binders. Nothing is kept of anything else. On iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device model has to agree what it is. This needs Binders 0.9.0 on your Mac: until Binders on your Mac is updated, your iPhone says so.
- Mode, under Said to yourself, says what happens next. Suggest, the default, offers each one with Add and Not Now, and once you've added nearly every one of a kind, adds that kind at once with Undo. Add at Once adds to-dos, ideas and notes at once, with Undo, and offers work for an agent with Give to. "Binders, …" is done at once, with Undo, in both.
- Auto mode adds what you say on your own at once, and hands a to-do or an idea straight to the agent Binders picks for it, or the one you name, without asking: a to-do to do, an idea to look into and plan. It also keeps a sentence without a cue, such as "the login times out too fast", when Binders' rules and Apple's on-device model both read it as something to do. Binders skips only its own question: each agent keeps its own permission checks and is told to ask your approval before it sends, posts or buys. Choosing Auto shows which agents may take what you say, and what the others need first.
- Auto mode hands over only to an agent you let start on cards Binders sends it, on your Mac, and said yes to on that iPhone. It hands over only what your iPhone heard as you on your own, and only once your decision model has been measured for it. It never hands anything to an agent that may send, post or buy for you, or that checks the board itself. Nothing said in a conversation with someone else, told to Siri or written in a note ever goes, and nothing goes while your iPhone plays audio as you speak. Each hand-over sends a notification with Stop, Undo and Not Me. Turn Off Auto is on the page a hand-over opens, and two Not Me's among the last ten turn it off for you.
- Thinking out loud. In Add at Once and Auto, a capture in which your iPhone heard only you is read for to-dos, fixes and ideas, each one on its own, and in Auto mode each is handed over with only the sentences it came from. Before you talk with someone, Wrap Up on the Lock Screen keeps what you thought out loud until then as only you. With Write down while the iPhone is locked on, what you think out loud goes a few minutes after you stop. A capture sends one notification for what it handed over, with Stop All, and Not Only Me takes back everything it made.
- Calibrate, under Said to yourself, tests those checks on your iPhone with phrases you read, the same read by someone else, and a TV or a podcast playing, then suggests the voice threshold and the bar Auto mode goes by. It keeps scores and timings, never audio or words, on the iPhone, until you delete them or export them as a CSV.
- Siri and Shortcuts. "Ask Binders" answers from your binders, with your iPhone unlocked. "Tell Binders" adds a to-do, an idea or a note, even with your iPhone locked, and never reads anything back. Both work from the Action Button too.
- A new home on iOS 26: Today, Binders and Capture, with one control at the bottom. Tap it to dictate, or hold it to ask: Binders shows your words as they're heard and asks when you let go. Ask also has a microphone button, and with Binders 0.9.0 on your Mac it shows the passages first, then the answer as it's written. A question asked aloud isn't kept with your dictations.
- On the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, a Live Activity shows that capture is listening and how many things were filed, as counts, with Mute and Wrap Up. There's an All-day Capture control for Control Center, the Lock Screen and the Action Button, and Today and Needs You widgets. Notifications about what you said say what was done, such as "To-do added in Personal", rather than your words, unless you turn on Show what you said on the Lock Screen in Capture settings.
- Write down while the iPhone is locked, in Capture settings, writes captures down in your pocket, on battery, within minutes. It's off until you turn it on, as it uses more battery. With it off, captures wait until you open Binders or plug in.
- With Binders 0.9.0 on your Mac, each capture says what became of it: Filed in a binder with its to-dos, Not kept (too short, small talk, or nothing to write down), or Not written up, with why. Its page has What happened, and Wrap Up Now closes a capture at once and keeps it from 3 seconds of speech. The capture screen shows today's kept captures, folds the rest into one row, and has Earlier captures; the gear opens Capture settings.
- Your Mac over any VPN you use on both, such as Covee or Tailscale. Your iPhone tries every address your Mac has told it about at once, and uses the first that answers. Tap Mac offline to see which were tried and what happened to each. Your Mac lists the addresses: test one, or add the name or address your VPN gives your Mac. On the Mac, Settings → iPhone lists the addresses it offers. An iPhone paired with Binders 0.8.0 or earlier learns your VPN's address the next time it connects at home. Forwarding a router's port to your Mac isn't supported.
- Agents from your iPhone. Give to hands a card, an offer from a capture, or something you said to an agent you set up on your Mac. The first time for each agent, Binders says what goes with the card, where, and who the agent sends it to, and asks first, and again when any of that changes. Answer an agent's question in its notification, and approve or decline a request on the card once your iPhone is unlocked.
- Dark by default, as on the Mac: Your Mac → This iPhone → Appearance has Light and Match System. A pill at the top shows while your Mac is connecting or out of reach, and the laptop button opens Your Mac. A screen with nothing on the iPhone yet says Not on this iPhone yet, with Try Again, instead of a spinner that never ended. Cleaning up a dictation takes a few seconds at most: past that, Binders' own rules tidy the words, and the sheet says who took too long.

## The Mac

- Settings has six pages: General, Dictation and writing, AI and knowledge, Capture, Agents and automations, and Devices and privacy. Each scrolls through its parts, and a link to one, such as Settings → MCP, opens it there.
- A new look on the Mac and the iPhone, with one meaning for each color: amber only for what needs you, red only while a microphone is live, lavender with the sparkle only for an agent's work, and green only for done. A binder's pages take a faint wash of its color. Notes follow light and dark mode. With Reduce Motion on, things fade instead of moving.
- Ask, on the Knowledge page and in Command Mode's panel, shows the passages with your words at once, then writes the answer as it comes. Changing the question, leaving the page or pressing Esc stops it. Dictation cleanup and answers now go ahead of capture write-ups on the language model, so they don't wait behind one.
- If Binders can't open its library, a strip across the window now says so, and nothing new is saved until you quit and reopen it: anything you try to save says why, instead of being lost at quit. Your iPhone keeps what you save until then, and AI tools are told Binders can't save right now.
- Pictures and videos from the web in a note or a card aren't loaded by themselves any more, so their site can't tell you opened it. Double-click one to open it in your browser. A web page pasted into a note fetches nothing.
- The keys your paired iPhones connect with are now kept in the macOS Keychain. They move there when you update, with no pairing again. If you go back to 0.8.0, pair your iPhone again.
- In a shared binder, a note an agent wrote or changed stays marked as the agent's for your teammates, until someone makes it theirs. Captures, and notes made from what you said to yourself near your iPhone, stay on your Mac even in a shared binder, and the Delete Binder dialog says so.

## For developers: MCP and automations

- New MCP tools: `list_agents`, the agents you hand cards to; `work_streams`, who is working on what, with the questions and reviews waiting on you; and `request_approval`, which asks you before an agent sends, posts, buys or deletes something, and brings your answer back on the card.
- `list_tasks` takes `for` and `since`, and `create_task` and `update_task` take `for`. Without a `binder`, `create_task` puts the card in the binder it fits best. Cards carry `state` in the words A2A and MCP Tasks share, `meant_for`, and `approval` while one waits on you.
- Any write tool takes `request_id`: sent again, the write is done once and gets the first answer. `list_todos` gives `origin` and `made_by`, notes give `made_by`, `edited_by` and `edited_at`, and `list_binders` gives `what_goes_here`. `append_to_note` and `update_note` on a note an agent didn't write mark it as edited by that agent.
- Captures, meeting transcripts and your teammates' items are left out for every tool until you allow them for it in Settings → MCP → What each tool can read. An entry Settings → MCP adds now names its tool (`BINDERS_AGENT`), and the board shows that name first, whatever an agent calls itself. After you update Binders, restart your AI tool or reconnect its Binders server.
- Automations. A To-do added rule runs for to-dos you made: in Command Mode, in the iPhone app, or with Add on a suggestion. Turn on Also for to-dos said to yourself, from agents and from links on a rule to have it run for those too: until you do, an existing rule no longer runs for to-dos from `binders://todo` links or MCP. `{todo_origin}`, `BINDERS_TODO_ORIGIN` and the web hook's `todo_origin` say where a to-do came from.
- A web hook's token is kept in your Keychain, not in automations.json, and goes as `Authorization: Bearer` only to the address the rule was saved with. Open a URL puts placeholders only in the query, encoded strictly, so what a to-do says can't change what the link opens. A template with one anywhere else is refused, and a rule saved earlier like that is refused when it runs, with the reason.
- `binders://card/<id>` opens a card in its own window, and `binders://note/<id>` opens a note in its binder. `binders://dictate`, `binders://capture/on` and `binders://meeting/start` now ask you first. An agent that's a web hook, a command or a Shortcut is told why it was woken in `event`: `ready`, `answered`, `commented`, `sent_back` or `manual`.

## Fixes

- Fixed: Binders could stall for a moment a few seconds after a save and every five minutes, and a save during a dictation or a meeting write-up could keep it busy until that ended. The search index now updates off the main thread.
- Fixed on the iPhone: dictation could wait without end for its last words. It now waits a few seconds and keeps what it heard, and a microphone that isn't free says so.
- Your iPhone notices quickly when your Mac goes to sleep, and while Binders is open it tries again as soon as the network or a VPN changes. With Binders 0.9.0 on your Mac, a note, to-do or answer whose reply was lost on the way is saved once, not twice.
- All-day capture on the iPhone: a call that only rings as a banner no longer pauses it, and if iOS stops Binders while it listens, a notification says so. A capture that fails loses its audio after its last try, not three days on, and one with no words in it loses it at once.
